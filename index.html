About Us

Food Affair Cold Chain Limited (also operating as Food Affair Logistics Co) is Nigeria's premier foodtech logistics company specializing in temperature-controlled transportation and storage solutions. Based in Abuja, we understand the critical importance of maintaining the cold chain for perishable goods in Nigeria's dynamic food industry.

Our CEO, a seasoned ghost kitchen operator, founded Food Affair with a deep understanding of the challenges faced by food businesses in preserving product quality during transit. We leverage cutting-edge refrigeration technology, GPS-tracked vehicles, and trained logistics professionals to bridge the gap between food producers and consumers.

Whether you're a restaurant chain, food manufacturer, pharmaceutical company, or agricultural exporter, we guarantee your products maintain optimal temperature from pickup to delivery across the FCT and beyond.
